# Email Drafting Tone Guide & Principles

Guidelines for generating context-aware email replies and new messages.

---

## 1. Tone Variants

Every draft generated should provide two distinct stylistic options:

### Variant A: Concise
- **Goal**: Direct, respectful of time, minimal fluff.
- **Length**: 2–4 sentences (under 100 words).
- **Best for**: Internal colleagues, quick confirmations, operational updates.
- **Format**:
  ```text
  Hi [Name],

  [Direct answer or status]. [Next step or question].

  Thanks,
  ```

### Variant B: Warmer / Detailed
- **Goal**: Relationship building, collaborative, comprehensive context.
- **Length**: 5–8 sentences (150–250 words).
- **Best for**: External clients, executive stakeholders, sensitive matters.
- **Format**:
  ```text
  Hi [Name],

  Thanks for reaching out regarding [Topic].

  [Context and rationale]. [Proposed solution or response].

  Please let me know if you would like to discuss further.

  Best regards,
  ```

---

## 2. Assumptions & Clarifying Questions

When facts are missing, **never fabricate commitments or dates**. Instead:
1. List explicit **Assumptions** (e.g. "Assuming budget is within Q3 limits").
2. Formulate 1–3 **Questions to Confirm** before finalizing the draft.
