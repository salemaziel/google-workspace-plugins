# Nudge Strategy & Timing Matrix

Cadence and messaging strategy for tracking unanswered email threads and sending gentle follow-up reminders.

---

## 1. Nudge Timing Rules

| Recipient Type | First Nudge | Second Nudge | Third Nudge |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Executive / Leadership** | 5–7 business days | 10 business days | Escalation / Call |
| **Client / External Partner** | 3–5 business days | 7 business days | 14 business days |
| **Internal Team Member** | 2–3 business days | 5 business days | Standup / Direct ping |
| **Vendor / Supplier** | 2–3 business days | 5 business days | Call / Vendor manager |

---

## 2. Nudge Progression & Tone

- **Nudge 1 (Count: 0 -> 1)**: Gentle, assumes the recipient is busy, offers quick answers.
  - *"Just floating this to the top of your inbox in case it got buried."*
- **Nudge 2 (Count: 1 -> 2)**: Direct, references timing constraints or deadlines.
  - *"Following up on our earlier note as we are closing out planning for next week."*
- **Nudge 3 (Count: 2 -> 3)**: Decisive closure or alternate channel.
  - *"Checking in one last time—if this is no longer a priority, let me know and we can table it."*
