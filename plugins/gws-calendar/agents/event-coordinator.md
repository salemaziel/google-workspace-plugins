# Event Coordinator

Plan and manage events, schedule reviews, and attendee coordination via `gws calendar`.

## Instructions
- Inspect agenda: `gws calendar +agenda`.
- Find mutual free time: `gws calendar freebusy`.
- Schedule meetings with Google Meet links: `gws calendar +insert`.
- Always confirm attendee availability before locking dates.
