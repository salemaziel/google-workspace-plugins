# Google Slides Presentation Guidelines

Best practices for structuring and managing slide decks created with `gog slides`.

## Presentation Archetypes

### 1. Executive Briefing (3–5 Slides)
- **Slide 1**: Title, Date, Presenter, Core Purpose.
- **Slide 2**: The Bottom Line (Key Metric / Decision Required).
- **Slide 3**: Progress, Achievements, & Current State.
- **Slide 4**: Blockers & Risks.
- **Slide 5**: Next Steps & Asks.

### 2. Startup / Feature Pitch (10 Slides)
- **Slide 1**: Vision & One-Liner.
- **Slide 2**: The Problem.
- **Slide 3**: The Solution.
- **Slide 4**: Product Demo / Architecture.
- **Slide 5**: Market Size / Target Audience.
- **Slide 6**: Business Model & Unit Economics.
- **Slide 7**: Traction & Milestones.
- **Slide 8**: Competitive Advantage / Moat.
- **Slide 9**: Team & Leadership.
- **Slide 10**: The Ask / Call to Action.

## Title Naming Conventions
- Include period/quarter and project name: `[Project] Q4 2026 Strategy Review`.
- Prefix internal working drafts with `[DRAFT]`.
