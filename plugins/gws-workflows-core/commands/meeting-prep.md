description: "Compile meeting preparation brief with agenda, attendees, and linked docs via gws workflow"
---

Prepare a comprehensive briefing dossier for your upcoming meeting.

## Usage
- `/meeting-prep`
- `/meeting-prep --next`

## Workflow
1. Execute the meeting prep workflow:
   ```bash
   gws workflow +meeting-prep
   ```
2. Extract:
   - Next scheduled meeting subject, time, and location/Meet link.
   - List of attendee names and email addresses.
   - Relevant Google Docs, Sheets, or Drive attachments.
   - Relevant email threads or past interaction notes.
3. Present structured dossier to ensure immediate meeting readiness.
